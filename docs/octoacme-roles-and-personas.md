# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Project Personas

These personas provide clear ownership for work that spans product discovery, delivery, measurement, and operations. Assign them when the project needs the expertise; on smaller projects, one person may cover multiple personas, but each responsibility and decision owner should remain explicit. They collaborate with the existing Product Manager, Project Manager, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders rather than replacing those roles.

### UX Designers / User Researchers

#### Role Summary
UX Designers and User Researchers understand user needs and shape accessible, usable experiences that support the product's goals.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share findings with the team
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Identify usability and accessibility needs for acceptance criteria
- Raise user experience risks and recommend evidence-based improvements

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to validate problems, refine requirements, and connect design choices to customer outcomes.
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and resolve implementation questions.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility scenarios in validation.
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager and Stakeholders to support planning and alignment.

### Technical Leads / Architects

#### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and help the delivery team make sound, maintainable engineering decisions.

#### Responsibilities
- Define or facilitate architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration needs
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and technical reviews
- Document significant decisions and communicate their delivery impact

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and technical risk mitigation.
- Work with the Project Manager to surface dependencies, sequencing needs, and estimate considerations.
- Partner with the Product Manager to explain technical trade-offs and clarify impacts on scope and outcomes.
- Consult QA/Testing and Stakeholders when design decisions affect quality, integrations, or operational needs.

### Data / Analytics Leads

#### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure that project outcomes can be measured with reliable, relevant evidence.

#### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into an instrumentation and measurement plan
- Define data requirements and coordinate their implementation
- Check data quality and analyze results after delivery
- Communicate findings, limitations, and recommendations

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define meaningful success metrics and interpret outcomes.
- Coordinate with Developers to implement and verify instrumentation.
- Work with QA/Testing to validate that measurement events and data are captured as expected.
- Share progress and findings with the Project Manager and Stakeholders to inform decisions and follow-up work.

### Security / Privacy Leads

#### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise the team on protecting systems and data, and on meeting applicable security and privacy requirements.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and data-handling risks early
- Advise on requirements, controls, and appropriate review checkpoints
- Coordinate or perform security and privacy reviews within their remit
- Escalate unresolved or material risks through the agreed governance path

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to make controls testable and verify relevant requirements.
- Advise the Product Manager on security and privacy implications of product scope and user data.
- Coordinate review timing and risk tracking with the Project Manager.
- Communicate material risks and required decisions to the Product Lead and relevant Stakeholders.

### Release / Operations Leads

#### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate production readiness and help ensure that services can be deployed, monitored, and recovered reliably.

#### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness needs, deployment coordination, and operational handoffs
- Ensure monitoring, support ownership, and rollback or mitigation plans are considered
- Coordinate post-deployment checks and communicate operational issues
- Capture operational learnings and feed them into follow-up work

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate readiness evidence and deployment needs with Developers and QA/Testing.
- Work with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, risks, and communications.
- Partner with the Product Manager to understand user impact and release priorities.
- Align operational expectations and support handoffs with Stakeholders and Customer Support.

### Customer Support / Service Representatives

#### Role Summary
Customer Support and Service Representatives bring customer-facing operational knowledge into planning and help prepare teams to support delivered changes.

#### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, pain points, and service trends
- Identify support impacts and readiness needs for planned changes
- Review customer-facing guidance and escalation paths before release
- Relay post-release feedback and emerging issues to the project team

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to represent customer needs and inform prioritization.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on support readiness, launch communications, and feedback follow-up.
- Partner with QA/Testing to include realistic customer support scenarios in acceptance and validation.
- Share relevant issues and guidance with Developers and Stakeholders, and coordinate handoffs with Release / Operations Leads.
